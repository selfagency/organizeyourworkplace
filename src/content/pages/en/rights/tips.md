**The boss can't threaten, interrogate, promise, or spy on you for organizing. Remember it as TIPS.**

## What this means for you

An unfair labor practice (a ULP — a complaint to the NLRB that your boss broke labor law) includes these four moves, aimed at union organizing or any protected group activity:

- **T — Threaten.** "Unionize and we close." "You'll lose your benefits." Threats of retaliation or closure because of organizing are illegal.
- **I — Interrogate.** "Are you one of them?" "Who's in the committee?" Coercive questioning about your own or coworkers' union activity is illegal.
- **P — Promise.** "Drop this and I'll get you a raise." Promising benefits to discourage organizing is illegal.
- **S — Spy.** Watching your union meetings, reading your organizing emails, or pretending to — is illegal. (Seeing open activity in areas supervisors normally walk is not spying.)

## What to do now

1. Write down every TIPS moment: date, time, who said what, who heard it. Notes from day one matter — evidence is now due within 2 weeks of filing a charge.
2. Don't argue in the moment. Say "I'd rather not discuss that," and walk away.
3. If it keeps happening, [file a ULP charge](/help/file-a-charge). You have 6 months from the violation.

## Watch out

The boss's lawyers know TIPS too. They train managers to stay just inside the line — "facts, opinions, examples" instead of threats. Learn [FOE](/rights/foe) so you can tell the difference.
