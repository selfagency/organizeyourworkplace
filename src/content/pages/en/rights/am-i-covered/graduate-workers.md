**Private-university grad workers who work — teaching and research — are employees under current Board law. The new Board majority may revisit that.**

## What this means for you

The Board has flip-flopped on grad workers for decades: covered (2000, Columbia 2016), not covered (2004, 2019), covered again (Columbia, 2016 — still the operative precedent). The current General Counsel has listed decisions she wants reversed, and the Board regained a Republican majority in 2026 [P1-1][P1-2].

What that means in practice: **your protection depends on precedent that may change while your campaign is running.** Universities know this and some are betting on it.

## What to do now

1. Move fast and build a big majority — the strongest protection against a rule change is a workforce the university can't ignore.
2. Contact [UAW Higher Ed](/unions) or another union that organizes private universities — they've run these campaigns through every flip-flop.
3. Watch [what's changed](/rights/whats-changed) — this is one of the rules most likely to move.

## Watch out

Public-university grad workers are state employees — outside the NLRA entirely. Your state's law (or your union's political strategy) is the route there. This site doesn't cover public-sector campaigns.
