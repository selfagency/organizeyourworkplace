**Some religious-employer workplaces can claim an exemption from the NLRA. Whether yours does depends on facts a lawyer should look at.**

## What this means for you

The NLRA excludes workers of a religious employer when the employer is operated **for religious purposes** and the workers are mainly engaged in religious activity. A church's office staff may be covered; a parochial school's teachers have been treated differently over the years depending on how much religious doctrine they teach.

The line is blurry and fact-specific. Courts and the Board have gone back and forth for decades.

## What to do now

1. Don't assume you're excluded. Talk to a worker-side lawyer or a worker center — [find one →](/help/lawyers).
2. If you're excluded from the NLRA, you still have routes: worker centers, direct action, and (in some states) state labor laws.
