**Signal, not SMS. A username, not your phone number. Disappearing messages on.**

## What this means for you

Signal is end-to-end encrypted (only you and the recipient can read it) and free. Set it up right:

1. **Install Signal** on your personal phone from the official app store.
2. **Set a username** and use it — coworkers reach you without seeing your number [A7].
3. **Turn on disappearing messages** in every organizing chat. Days, not weeks.
4. **Two groups, not one:** a **Committee** group (the 3–7 core) and a **Trusted contacts** group (wider, less sensitive). Signal groups support admin approval and admin-only timer control [A17].
5. **Admin approval on.** New members join by invite, and an admin approves each one.
6. **Screen lock** on, and register a Signal PIN.

## What to do now

1. Set up your Signal account tonight, before the first one-on-one.
2. Create the Committee group when your [committee](/organize/committee) forms.
3. Never move organizing chats to SMS or WhatsApp.

## Watch out

Signal keeps no record of group membership, names or avatars — but your *phone* does. See [if your phone is taken](/organize/security/if-your-phone-is-taken).
