**Natural leaders aren't the loudest people. They're the ones coworkers go to.**

## What this means for you

Every workplace has people whose opinion moves others. They're rarely the most vocal. Signs of a leader:

- Coworkers ask them questions: how does the PTO policy work, have you dealt with this manager.
- They're good at their job, or good at protecting others from bad rules.
- When they complain, others nod.

The boss also knows who they are — which is why leaders often get the first raise, the best schedule, and the friendliest treatment when organizing starts. Watch for it.

## What to do now

1. From your [map](/organize/mapping), mark who others go to.
2. Prioritize one-on-ones with them. A leader who says yes moves ten people. A leader who says no moves ten the other way.
3. Don't skip the quiet ones because they're quiet. Quiet leaders are the best ones.

## Watch out

Don't confuse friendly with trusted. The person who jokes with everyone may be liked; the person who fixed someone's schedule mess is trusted. Trust moves votes.
