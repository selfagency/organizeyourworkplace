## What we know so far

- The law treats remote workers the same: talking with coworkers about pay and conditions is protected, on Slack or on Signal.
- The practice is different: no break room, no parking lot, no walking out together. The one-on-one happens on video or voice, and the "map" is a spreadsheet of who's in which team.
- Company chat is company property. Organize on personal channels.

## What to do now
