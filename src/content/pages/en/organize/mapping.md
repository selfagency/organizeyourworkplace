**You can't win a campaign you can't see. Map every coworker, department and shift.**

## What this means for you

Mapping answers three questions:

- **Who works here?** Every department, every shift, every job.
- **Where does the power sit?** Who influences whom. Who does everyone go to when something breaks.
- **Where is the anger?** Which departments are hottest, and about what.

The tool for this is the **wall chart**: every coworker by department and shift, with a support rating from **Strong yes / Leaning yes / Unsure / Leaning no / Against**. The words, not just a number — a leaked chart full of "1s and 2s" reads as contempt.

## What to do now

1. List every department and shift from memory. Fill gaps as you learn.
2. After each conversation, update the chart. Use the [wall chart template](/toolkit/wall-chart) — it lives on your devices, encrypted, never on a server.
3. Rate honestly. A chart full of yeses feels good and lies to you.

## Watch out

The wall chart is the most dangerous artifact you'll create. If it leaks, everyone on it is exposed. That's why it lives in the encrypted workspace, not in a spreadsheet on a work laptop. See [security](/organize/security).
