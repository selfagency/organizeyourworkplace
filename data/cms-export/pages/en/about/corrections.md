**Found something wrong — a number, a legal claim, a dead union link? Tell us. No account, no form tracking.**

## What this means for you

This site publishes fast-moving legal and contact information. Errors will happen; what matters is how fast they die. Corrections are reviewed by a human, fixed in the source records, and — for legal pages — logged in the [changelog](/rights/whats-changed) with the date.

Until the channel ships: use the project repository's issue tracker — the link in the site footer. Include:

1. The page URL and the exact claim you're disputing.
2. What's wrong and your source, if you have one.
3. Whether it's urgent (someone could be hurt by it) — say so at the top.

## What we won't do

- We won't publish your name or email anywhere, ever.
- We won't argue legal strategy over corrections — see [lawyers and worker centers](/help/lawyers) for advice about your case.
- We won't take down verified factual claims because a party dislikes them; we will fix any factual error found.
