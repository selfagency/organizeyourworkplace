**The honest version: here is everything this site collects, keeps, and never touches. If this page and the deployment ever disagree, believe neither until they match.**

## What the site keeps

- **Content pages only — basic page counts** through our own analytics, served from the same server. No IPs, no query strings, no cross-site tracking, and we never follow you to other sites. Counts are kept about 30 days.
- **Error reports** (only if something breaks) go to our own error tracker, kept 14 days, with IPs stripped. Tool pages don't send error reports unless you explicitly tap send.
- **No accounts anywhere.** No email, no passwords, nothing to sign up for.

## What the tools never send

Every tool — the [union matcher](/unions/matcher), the [deadline calculator](/toolkit/deadlines), the wallet card — runs entirely in your browser. Your answers, your boss's name, your notes: **they never leave your device.** There is no server to send them to.

## What to assume anyway

- **Do not use work devices for organizing.** This site can't protect you from your employer's Wi-Fi, DNS logs, or MDM. The banner on your first visit says the same.
- **The leaving-site warning exists** because we can't control what other sites record once you leave.
- **This page describes what's deployed.** When the configuration changes, this page changes with it — the honest policy is the one that matches reality.

## What to do now

1. Read [security for organizers](/organize/security) — protecting yourself is mostly about your device, not this site.
2. If something here looks wrong for the site you're looking at, [report it](/about/corrections) — it matters.
