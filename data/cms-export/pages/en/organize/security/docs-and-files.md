**Plans, lists and charts live encrypted, on your devices — never on a work laptop, never in a plain Google Doc.**

## What this means for you

Your wall chart, one-on-one notes and plans are the most sensitive documents of the campaign. Rules:

1. **Never on a work device or work account.** No work Google Drive, no work OneDrive, no work laptop "just for tonight."
2. **Encrypted and local-first.** The [Committee Workspace](/toolkit) keeps the wall chart and notes end-to-end encrypted on members' own phones — servers only relay scrambled data.
3. **Until the workspace launches (R2):** a shared CryptPad (end-to-end encrypted, self-hosted) is the fallback. Ask your union or EWOC for access.
4. **Back up monthly** to your own storage, encrypted.

## What to do now

1. Move anything sensitive off work systems today.
2. When the workspace is live, your committee gets one-time invites — no accounts, no emails.
3. Keep personal notes in your phone's notes app only if the phone is encrypted and locked (it should be).
