**Assume the boss can see everything on company systems. So never touch them.**

## What this means for you

The most common way campaigns die isn't a spy — it's a coworker reporting to the boss, or an organizer getting sloppy on a work device. Company email, Slack, laptops, phones and Wi-Fi can all be monitored by IT [A1].

**The rules:**

1. **Never use company systems for organizing.** Not email, not Slack, not the work laptop, not work Wi-Fi for anything sensitive.
2. **Your own phone, your own accounts.** Signal, not SMS. Personal email, not work email.
3. **Assume anything digital can leak.** Keep the wall chart and plans in the encrypted workspace, not in a spreadsheet.
4. **Vet before you share names.** Technology can't fix a coworker who reports to the boss. One-on-ones and time are the vetting.
5. **Build a security plan.** EFF's Surveillance Self-Defense guide has a step-by-step security-plan exercise — use it [A1].
