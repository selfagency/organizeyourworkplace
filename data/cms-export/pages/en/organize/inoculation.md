**The boss's campaign is predictable. Tell your coworkers what's coming before it arrives.**

## What this means for you

When organizing surfaces, the boss runs a script: letters from leadership, "concern" meetings, sudden raises, captive-audience speeches, a consultant running the show. It's so predictable that organizers call it a playbook.

**Inoculation** is the vaccine: before the boss speaks, you speak. "In the next few weeks, you'll probably get an email from [manager] about the union. Here's what it will say, and here's what's true."

Why it works: the first story people hear sticks. If your coworker hears your honest version first, the boss's version lands as the second story — and gets compared, not believed.

## What to do now

1. List the boss's likely moves: the letter, the meetings, the promises, the "we're a family" speech.
2. For each, write the honest answer. The [answers page](/vote/answers) has the top 15.
3. Deliver it in one-on-ones, before the boss's version arrives. Use the [inoculation builder](/organize/inoculation).

## Watch out

Don't predict specific punishments you can't back up. Inoculate with what's *likely*, not what's scary. Overclaiming burns your credibility — and credibility is the whole game.
