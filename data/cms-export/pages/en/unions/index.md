**Most industries have 2–3 unions that could organize you. Here's how to find yours.**

## What this means for you

You don't pick a union off a shelf. You find the ones that organize your industry, talk to them, and pick the one that shows up. The profiles below are generated from our verified directory — intake links appear only when we've confirmed them.

If you don't know where to start:

- **[EWOC](/unions/no-union-yet)** gives free volunteer coaching to any worker, any industry. They've heard from 5,000+ workers since 2020 [P1-8].
- **The AFL-CIO** routes you to the right affiliate [P1-9].

## What to do now

1. Use the [matcher](/unions/matcher) — answer a few questions, get your likely unions.
2. Read [how to pick](/unions/how-to-pick) — 10 questions to ask before you commit.
3. Make [the first call](/unions/first-call) — here's what happens when you do.

## Watch out

We list unions neutrally and rank by verified organizing activity, not opinion. An intake link you see here has been checked against the union's own site.
