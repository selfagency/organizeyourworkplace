**Answer a few questions about your workplace. Get the unions that organize your industry.**

## What this means for you

The matcher runs entirely on your phone — your answers never leave the browser. It uses the same verified directory as the [profiles page](/unions/profiles).

## What to do now

Open the [matcher tool](/unions/matcher). It walks you through:

1. What kind of work you do.
2. What kind of employer you work for.
3. Where you are.

Then it shows your likely unions, with verified contact links where they exist.

## Watch out

The matcher suggests; it doesn't decide. Two or three unions may fit. Talk to more than one before you commit — see [how to pick](/unions/how-to-pick).
