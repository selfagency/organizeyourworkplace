**Every union started with workers nobody would help. Free coaching exists. Start there.**

## What this means for you

Your industry isn't in the directory? Your shop is too small, too new, too weird for the big unions? That's not a dead end. Two organizations exist exactly for this:

- **[EWOC — Emergency Workplace Organizing Committee](https://workerorganizing.org/support/).** Volunteer organizers from the DSA and UE who coach any worker, any industry, any state — free. They've heard from thousands of workers since 2020 and helped win first contracts in industries with no union tradition [P1-8].
- **[AFL-CIO organizer referral](https://aflcio.org/contact-union-organizer).** The federation's contact form routes you to the affiliate that should be organizing your kind of workplace — including ones that don't advertise it [P1-9].

## What to do now

1. **Contact EWOC.** Tell them your job, your city, and your issue. You'll get a real volunteer organizer, a plan, and weekly check-ins.
2. **Also use the AFL-CIO form** if you want a traditional union path. More than one conversation is normal — you're hiring help.
3. **Start organizing now, before any call.** Everything in [Organize](/organize) — the first conversation, the map, the committee — works with zero union support. The workers who get coached furthest are the ones who already started.

## Watch out

You do not need a union's permission to organize. You already have the [legal right](/rights/section-7) to act together with your coworkers. The union comes later; the committee comes first.
