**Yes. You can talk with your coworkers about pay, hours, and safety — with or without a union.**

## What this means for you

The National Labor Relations Act (the main federal labor law) has a Section 7. It says workers can act together to improve pay and conditions. Talking about them counts.

You do not need a union for this protection. Two coworkers comparing pay on a break is protected. So is asking others to sign a petition about safety.

The National Labor Relations Board (NLRB, the government agency that enforces this law) handles complaints.

## What to do now

1. Talk about work problems with your coworkers — pay, schedules, safety.
2. Do it on breaks, in person, on your own phone. Never on company email or Slack.
3. If the boss punishes you for it, that may be illegal. See [what to do today](/help/retaliation).
