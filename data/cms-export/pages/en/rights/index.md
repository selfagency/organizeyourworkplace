**You have legal rights at work right now, union or not. Start here.**

## What this means for you

The National Labor Relations Act protects workers who act together about pay, hours, and conditions. You don't need a union for that protection — but you do need to be covered by the law, and you need to know where its limits are.

The law is moving fast right now. Every page in this section shows its as-of date. Check [what's changed](/rights/whats-changed) before you rely on a rule.

## What to do now

1. Read [Section 7](/rights/section-7) — the core protection.
2. Learn [TIPS](/rights/what-the-boss-cant-do) — what the boss can't do.
3. Learn [FOE](/rights/what-the-boss-can-do) — what the boss *can* do, and how to answer it.
4. Check [am I covered?](/rights/am-i-covered)
