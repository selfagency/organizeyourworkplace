**Most private-sector workers are covered. Government workers are not. Check yours.**

## What this means for you

The National Labor Relations Act covers **private-sector employees** — including nonprofit employees — once the employer is big enough to meet the NLRB's dollar thresholds. Nonprofits are judged by the same standards as for-profit businesses. For example, NPEU represents staff at more than 50 organizations, including the ACLU, Brookings and the Urban Institute [P1-7].

**Not covered by the NLRA:**

- **Government workers** (federal, state, county, city, public schools). Some states have their own laws — but this site doesn't cover them. Contact a union or a worker center in your state.
- **Agricultural and domestic workers.**
- **Supervisors and independent contractors** (with narrow exceptions).
- **Airlines and rail** — covered, but by a different law (the Railway Labor Act). [See that page →](/rights/am-i-covered/airlines-and-rail)
