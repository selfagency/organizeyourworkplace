**You'll need two things in every conversation: facts that hold up and words that land. This section has both.**

## What this means for you

Union membership isn't fringe — **10% of US wage and salary workers are union members, 14.7 million people** [A85]. And unions pay: nonunion median weekly earnings are **84% of union earnings** — $1,174 versus $1,404 [A85]. (That's a raw comparison, not a controlled study — the [numbers page](/case/numbers) handles the honest version, including the EPI analysis of the union wage premium [P1-12].)

The stronger case, though, isn't statistics — it's your workplace's story: what changed, what the boss did, what we can do together. Facts earn the right to be heard; stories get believed.

## Watch out

Don't drown people in data. One number that answers the question beats ten that sound like homework.
