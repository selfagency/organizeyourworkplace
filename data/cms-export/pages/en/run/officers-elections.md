**Every officer position is elected, by secret ballot, on a clock the law sets. Steal an election and you lose more than an officer — you lose the union.**

## What this means for you

Title IV of the LMRDA sets the minimum rules for union officer elections [A82][A83]:

- **Time limits:** elections at least every **5 years** in national/international unions, **4 years** in intermediate bodies, **3 years** in local unions [A82].
- **Secret ballot** among members in good standing. International and intermediate bodies may elect by convention delegates chosen by secret ballot [A82].
- **Every member in good standing has the right** to nominate candidates, run (subject to reasonable, uniformly applied qualifications), and vote [A83].
- **Campaign rights:** candidates may distribute campaign material at their own expense; unions may not discriminate against candidates in use of membership lists; members on dues-checkoff can't be barred from voting over alleged dues payment delays [A83].
- **Notice:** election notice goes to every member's home address at least **15 days** before [A83].
- **No union or employer money for campaigns.** Union funds may pay only the costs of running the election itself [A83].
- **Records preserved for one year**; members who exhaust internal remedies (or get no decision within 3 months) can complain to the Secretary of Labor within a month after [A83].

## What to do now

1. **Put the whole election in your [bylaws](/run/bylaws):** nomination window, campaign period, ballot, counting, challenges.
2. **Run it like the NLRB election you fought:** wide notice, real contest, independent counting. Momentum from your organizing campaign is not a mandate to skip elections.
3. **Train election committee members** on the rules above — they enforce them.
4. **Log everything:** notices, ballots, tallies, protests. The one-year record rule [A83] exists because challenges happen.

## Watch out

An uncontested election is legal — but a habit of them is a symptom. If the same 5 people hold every office forever, your [keep organizing](/run/keep-organizing) work is failing.
