**You won the contract. Now the harder fight: making the union run for years — run by members, not by whoever shows up.**

## What this means for you

New unions die two ways: the boss grinds them down (that's what the contract fights), or they hollow out from inside — meetings with 4 people, elections nobody notices, stewards nobody trusts. Both are normal. Both are survivable with boring, consistent habits: real meetings, real elections, real money records.

Federal law gives you the floor to build on. The LMRDA — the law governing private-sector unions — guarantees members a bill of rights: free speech, fair elections, protection against improper discipline, and the right to see the union's finances [A83].

## What to do now

1. Write your [bylaws](/run/bylaws) — the rulebook that keeps the union yours.
2. Run clean [officer elections](/run/officers-elections) — the law sets the floor: secret ballot, time limits, member rights [A82].
3. Get [dues and money](/run/dues-and-money) right from day one — transparency is a legal duty and the trust of the whole project.
4. Train [stewards](/run/stewards) and learn [Weingarten rights](/run/stewards) — the contract only works if someone enforces it.
5. Set up the [grievance process](/run/grievances) and never stop [organizing](/run/keep-organizing).

## Watch out

Don't build dues collection here — this site will never process money. See [dues and money](/run/dues-and-money) for how to set it up with standard membership software.
