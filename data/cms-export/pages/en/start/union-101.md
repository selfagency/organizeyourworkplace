**A union is you and your coworkers acting together — with a contract behind you.**

## What this means for you

A union is not a third party. It is the workers. You elect your officers, you vote on your contract, you decide whether to strike.

What you get: a contract with real rules on pay, schedules and safety. Union workers earn more — median weekly pay is $1,404 for union members vs. $1,174 for nonunion [P1-12].

What a union is not: a company that takes dues and disappears. Dues only exist after workers vote yes, and members set them.

## What to do now

1. Look at the problems you and your coworkers share.
2. Read [how to start the first conversation](/organize/first-conversation).
3. When you're ready, [find a union](/unions) that organizes your industry.

## Watch out

The boss will say the union is an outside third party. It's the most common argument, and it's false. See [answers to the boss's arguments](/vote/answers).
