**Every winning campaign walks these 8 stages. You're at stage 1.**

## The stages

1. **Get angry** — name the problem. Pay? Schedules? Safety? Respect? Write it down.
2. **Talk to coworkers** — one-on-one, private, on your own time. Listen first. [How →](/organize/first-conversation)
3. **Build a committee** — the 3–7 coworkers everyone trusts. This is your leadership team. [How →](/organize/committee)
4. **Find a union** — or decide to go independent. [Matcher →](/unions/matcher)
5. **Go public / sign cards** — the committee goes public together. Cards show support. [What cards mean →](/vote/cards)
6. **Win the vote** — the boss fights back. Expect it. Prepare for it. [The playbook, decoded →](/vote/boss-campaign)
7. **Win the contract** — the election isn't the finish line. [Why →](/contract/first-contract)
8. **Run the union** — elect officers, keep organizing. [How →](/run/index)

## What this means for you

You don't need to know how to do stage 8 to start stage 1. Each stage has its own page, its own tools, and its own risks. Most campaigns take months, not weeks. Some take years.
