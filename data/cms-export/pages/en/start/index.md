**You're not powerless. You and your coworkers can fix this — together.**

## What this means for you

Your boss counts on you feeling alone. The law that protects you, the National Labor Relations Act, protects *workers acting together* — not lone complainers. That's the whole trick: you don't need to be brave alone. You need two or three coworkers who feel the same way.

Most organizing drives start exactly where you are: one person, angry, sure they'll get fired if they say anything. Then they talk to one coworker. Then another.

## What to do now

1. **Answer 5 quick questions** and the [router](/toolkit/router) tells you where to start. (Runs on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.)
2. **Read the [8 stages](/start/stages)** from angry to contract, so you know the road.
3. **Talk to one coworker you trust.** Not about "a union" — about the thing that made you angry. [How to start that conversation →](/organize/first-conversation)

## Watch out

Do this on your own phone, not a work device. Work email, work Slack, and work Wi-Fi can all be monitored. If you're on one right now, close this and come back on your own phone.
