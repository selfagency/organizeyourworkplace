**From filing to election is measured in weeks, not months. That's by design — and it means your organizing has to be done before you file.**

## What this means for you

The 2023 NLRB election rules restored a fast pre-election process [A70]. A typical uncontested case runs like this:

| Step | What happens | Rough clock |
|---|---|---|
| Petition filed | RC petition + showing of interest to the regional office | day 0 |
| Notice posted | Employer must post the Notice of Petition for Election | shortly after filing |
| Pre-election hearing | Only if issues are disputed (unit, timing, bars) | about a week after service |
| Statement of position | Employer lists its disputes | by the hearing |
| Final voter list | Employer gives the union names + contacts | within 2 days of the election order |
| Election | Secret ballot at the worksite | days to a few weeks out — "earliest practicable" |
| Objections window | Either side may contest conduct | 7 days after the tally |
| Certification | NLRB certifies the result | after objections resolve |

Contested cases — unit fights, eligibility battles, ULP charges — stretch the calendar. That's often the point of them.

## What to do now

1. **Do the math backwards.** If the election is ~4 weeks after filing, and the boss's campaign peels off supporters from day one, you need your majority deep enough to survive the whole ride. See [why 70%](/vote/petition).
2. **Use the deadline calculator** to see your own dates — it's local-only, nothing is sent anywhere.
3. **Plan campaign coverage for every week** between filing and voting: inoculation for new hires, check-ins for soft supporters, visibility actions. See [the boss's playbook](/vote/boss-campaign).
4. **Watch the objections window.** If the boss cheated, evidence is due fast — see [file a charge](/help/file-a-charge).

## Watch out

Timelines shift with NLRB practice and litigation over the 2023 rules [A70]. Your organizer will know the current real-world pace in your region. Don't file before your committee and your list are ready — the clock is the boss's friend if you're not.
