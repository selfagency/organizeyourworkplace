**When a majority of your coworkers want the union, you have two roads: the boss recognizes you, or you win an NLRB election. Both end the same way — at the bargaining table.**

## What this means for you

There are two ways to get legal union status:

1. **Voluntary recognition.** You show the boss that most of the unit wants the union. The boss signs a paper saying so. Fast, no election, no campaign season for the boss to attack you. Since the 2023 Cemex decision, a boss who gets a majority request generally must either recognize the union or file for an election within two weeks [A74].
2. **NLRB election.** You file a petition with the NLRB and win a secret-ballot vote. Official, certified, harder for the boss to challenge later — but it means weeks of open campaigning while the boss does his worst.

Most drives try recognition first. Most bosses refuse — which is fine, because the petition route is always there.
