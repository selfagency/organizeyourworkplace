**The election is a secret ballot run by the NLRB, not the boss and not the union. Here's exactly how it goes.**

## What this means for you

When the Regional Director orders the election, the parties agree on date, time, place, ballot language, and who's eligible. If they can't agree, the NLRB sets it all [A68].

- **The vote is secret.** Neither the boss nor anyone else learns how anyone voted [A68].
- **The employer must post the final election notice** at least two days before the vote, and give the union a final voter list with contact info within two days of the election order [A70].
- **Observers:** each side may have an observer at the polls — usually a worker. They verify eligibility but can't talk to voters or how anyone voted.
- **Challenged ballots:** if eligibility is disputed, the person votes anyway and the ballot is sealed as "challenged" — counted later only if it matters.
- **Runoffs:** in a three-way race (two unions or union + "no union") with no majority, the top two go to a runoff [A68].
- **The count:** ballots are counted at the site right after polls close, and you can watch. The tally is posted by the NLRB.

If either side objects to conduct, it has **seven days after the tally** to file objections [A70].

## What to do now

1. **Turnout is everything.** A low-turnout election amplifies the boss's most scared employees. Treat election day like a get-out-the-vote operation: confirm every supporter has a plan to vote.
2. **Name your observers** — steady, respected workers, one per shift if voting spans shifts.
3. **Don't campaign in the polling area** — rules against electioneering near the polls are strict, and a violation can overturn the result either way.
4. **If you win:** the NLRB certifies the union and the boss must bargain in good faith. Go straight to [the first contract](/contract/first-contract) — that fight starts immediately.
5. **If you lose:** objections or charges may apply if the boss cheated; otherwise the union can generally file a new petition later. A loss with 45% builds the next win.

## Watch out

Don't celebrate in front of anyone on the fence before the count is official, and don't discuss people's votes — the secret ballot protects everyone, including you.
