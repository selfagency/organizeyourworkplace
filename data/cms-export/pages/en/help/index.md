**Something already went wrong? This section is for right now.**

## What this means for you

You don't need a union — or a lawyer — to act. Federal law protects you, and the agency that enforces it is free.

- **Retaliated against?** Start at [fired or punished: do this today](/help/retaliation). The clock matters: charges generally must be filed within six months, and evidence within two weeks of filing.
- **Want to file a charge?** [Step by step](/help/file-a-charge) — free, and NLRB staff will help you write it.
- **Need your regional office?** The [NLRB office finder](/help/nlrb-office) lists all of them with phone numbers.
- **Need a lawyer?** [Worker-side lawyers and legal aid](/help/lawyers) — directories that actually work for workers.
- **Want support without a lawyer?** [Worker centers and free coaching](/help/worker-centers).

## Watch out

Deadlines in this section are hard deadlines — the six-month rule is statutory [A71], and since late 2025 the NLRB can dismiss a charge whose evidence arrives late [A79]. When in doubt, file and talk to the NLRB information officer the same day.

*Not legal advice. For advice about your situation, see [lawyers and worker centers](/help/lawyers).*
