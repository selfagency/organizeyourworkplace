**Retaliation for organizing is illegal. The NLRB can get you reinstated with back pay. But the clock starts now.**

## What this means for you

If you were fired, disciplined, demoted, had hours cut, or got threatened for supporting the union or acting with coworkers, that is an **unfair labor practice** — illegal under the National Labor Relations Act [A71]. Filing a charge is free, you don't need a lawyer, and anyone can file — you don't have to be the person targeted [A71].

Two deadlines rule everything:

- **Six months** from the event to file a charge — it's the law, and it's strict [A71].
- **Two weeks** after filing to submit your evidence: a timeline, documents, and a witness list with contact info. Since late 2025, missing the evidence window can get the charge dismissed for "lack of cooperation" [A79].

## What to do now — today

1. **Write everything down now, while it's fresh.** Dates, times, exact words, who was present, who said what. Memories decay fast and the two-week window is coming.
2. **Get off work systems.** No evidence lives on a work phone, work email, or work Wi-Fi. Personal device only.
3. **Save the evidence in the two weeks you have:** write-ups, schedules before and after, the firing letter, messages, names and phone numbers of witnesses.
4. **Call your NLRB regional office today** — find it in the [office finder](/help/nlrb-office). Ask for an information officer. They help free of charge and can help draft the charge [A73].
5. **Talk to your committee before going public**, not after. Coworkers who saw it are witnesses now; they may be targets next. See [security basics](/organize/security).
6. **Check the full deadline picture** in the [deadline calculator](/toolkit/deadlines).

## Watch out

Do not sign anything the boss hands you "just to close it out" — severance agreements can waive your right to pursue the claim. You are allowed to say "I need to review this first." And keep organizing: a charge is one tool, but the strongest protection for everyone is a committee that stays visible.

*Not legal advice. For advice about your situation, see [lawyers and worker centers](/help/lawyers).*
