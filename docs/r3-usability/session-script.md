# R3.0 usability sessions — script for the facilitator

**Who runs these: humans (owner + volunteers). Who made the materials: the agent.**
5 sessions, each 25–30 minutes, one participant at a time, on a phone-sized paper
prototype (or the HTML prints below on a phone-width screen). Participants:
non-organizers who match the audience — coworkers who might join a drive, not
organizers or tech people. Pay or thank-you gift recommended.

## Setup

- Print each prototype at phone scale (~9×16 cm) or open the HTML files on a phone.
- Cut the "rating chips" (1 Strong yes … 5 Against with words), issue tags
  (pay/schedule/safety/respect), and blank cards.
- Record with permission (audio enough); notes template at the end.

## Session flow (25 min)

### 1. Warm-up (3 min)
"Think about your job. If you could change one thing tomorrow, what would it be?"
(Listen for issues vocabulary — do their words match our issue tags?)

### 2. Screen 1 — Today (4 min)
Hand the prototype. "You open the app the morning of drive day 12. What do you
look at first? What does this screen tell you?"
Watch for: do they understand the majority meter (now/30%/70%) without being
told? Do they notice the "no one is talking to these coworkers" number?
**Fail signal:** they ask "what's 30%?" — the meter labels need words, not bars.

### 3. Screen 2 — Wall chart (5 min)
"Your committee tracks every coworker here. Show me where night-shift people
are. Who haven't you contacted in two weeks?"
Give them the filter chips as cut-outs (Strong+leaning, Unassigned, 14+ days).
Watch for: grid vs explorer comprehension; whether the dot-per-person reads at
all; whether 1–5 ratings read as a scale or as random colors (chips have words —
confirm they USE the words, not the colors).

### 4. Screen 3 — Coworker card (4 min)
"This is one person's card." "What did the committee do with this person?
When do you follow up?"
Watch for: rating history comprehension; whether next-step + due date reads as
an obligation or decoration.

### 5. Screen 4 — Log a conversation (5 min)
Role-play: "You just talked to Rosa in dairy. She went from unsure to leaning
yes — the schedule is her issue. Log it."
They move the rating chip old→new, pick the issue tag, write a next step.
**Fail signal:** they hesitate on "rating" language. Test the alternative:
"Where are they now?" — record exact words that work.

### 6. Trust probes (3 min)
"Where do you think this is stored?" (Expect wrong answers; we're testing copy.)
Show this line: *"No server holds your data. Our servers pass along scrambled
messages they can't read. They can see that a device connected, and when."*
"Does that promise make sense? Is it too nerdy? What would you tell a
suspicious coworker?"

### 7. Wrap (1 min)
"What's the one thing you'd delete from all this?"

## Capture template (per session)

```
Participant: (role/industry, no names needed) ___
Meter understood without help: y/n
Wall chart view preferred: grid / explorer / confused
Rating language that worked: ___
Card comprehension: ok / gaps: ___
Log-flow hesitation points: ___
Trust-copy reaction (exact words): ___
One thing to delete: ___
```

## After the 5 sessions

Facilitator sends the 5 capture sheets back. The agent turns findings into
screen-1–4 copy/layout changes before R3.8 builds them. Anything structural
(a view that fails for everyone) goes to `06-open-decisions.md`.
